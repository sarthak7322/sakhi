How it works

Four sentences. One complete record.

No forms, no typing, no screen-staring while walking between houses.

  1. 01

    Say who

    “सुनीता देवी, ID 1001, ANC visit” — the register fills her name, weeks of pregnancy and vaccines due.

  2. 02

    Say the readings

    “वज़न 58 किलो, BP 120 by 80, Hb 10.5” — every value confirmed out loud, odd ones questioned.

  3. 03

    Hear it back

    “बस, save करो” — Sakhi reads the whole record back. Change anything by just saying it.

  4. 04

    Saved, with next steps

    “हाँ सही है” — saved with follow-ups: PHC referral, vaccines due, the next home visit.

Safety first

A danger sign is never just a note.

Bleeding, fits, a baby not feeding, fast breathing, BP 140/90 or more — the moment it is said, Sakhi speaks a fixed “refer to PHC now” message, flags the record and puts it at the top of the follow-up list.

  • Three detectors in parallel: keywords (with negation), measured numbers, and the AI
  • Signs cited from the MCP card, HBNC and IMNCI guidelines
  • Never diagnoses, never suggests a medicine or dose
  • Nothing is saved until the record is read back and she says “हाँ”
  • Synthetic beneficiaries only · audio is never stored

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is any real patient data used?

No. The register holds synthetic beneficiaries only. Audio is streamed for transcription and never stored; a text-only voice log is kept for review.

Does Sakhi give medical advice?

Never. She fills the register. For danger signs she says one fixed line — refer to the PHC now — and for medical questions she asks the ASHA to consult the ANM or PHC doctor.

What if she mishears a number?

Every value is repeated back as it is captured, unusual values (a 28 kg newborn, BP 80/120) are questioned, and the whole record is read back before saving. Any field can also be corrected by tapping it.

Which languages work?

Hindi, English, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam, with Indic–English code-mixing. The fastest local understanding currently covers Hindi, Hinglish and English.

What happens when the internet or AI is slow?

Routine dictation is understood locally, so most of a visit needs no AI call at all. If the AI is unavailable, Sakhi keeps every value she understood and asks for one thing at a time.

How do I try it?

Open the app, pick a language, allow the microphone and start talking — or press ▶ Demo to hear a scripted visit.
