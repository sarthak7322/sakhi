      ❓ How to use Sakhi

      1. Say who: “Sunita Devi, ID 1001, ANC visit”
      2. Say the readings: “वज़न 58 किलो, BP 120 by 80, Hb 10.5”
      3. Correct by voice: “नहीं, वज़न 56 किलो है”, or tap the field
      4. Finish: “बस, save करो”. Sakhi reads it all back
      5. Confirm: “हाँ सही है”. Saved, with follow-ups

      Danger signs (bleeding, fits, not feeding, fast breathing, high BP…) are referred to the PHC at once. Sakhi never gives medicines or a diagnosis. You can interrupt her at any time.
